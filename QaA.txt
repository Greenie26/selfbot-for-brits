.question what are the essential commands
Essential commands include:
- /info: for general information.
- /hub: to teleport to the hub.
- /q: to open the quest menu.
- /s or /st: to access the skill tree.
- /sell: to sell items to the server.
- /qr: to open virtual quarries.
- /tod: to check the current time.
- /outpost, /bandit, /tower1-6: for teleporting to specific locations.
- /sethome <name>, /home remove <name>, /home list, /home help: for managing homes.
- /tpr <player>, /tpa, /tpb, /tpc: for sending and accepting teleport requests.

These commands are crucial for navigating and progressing in the game.
