/info -: Shows information.
/hub -: Teleports to hub.
/q -: Opens quest menu.
/s -: Opens stock market (shop).
/st -: Opens skill tree.
/sell -: Sells items to the server.
/qr -: Opens virtual quarries.
/tod -: Prints current time.
/wipe -: Shows next wipe date.
/outpost -: Teleports to outpost.
/bandit -: Teleports to bandit camp.
/tower1-6 -: Teleports to public heli towers (must be in a regular world).
/tpr <player> -: Sends a teleport request.
/tpa -: Accepts a teleport request.
/tpb -: Teleports back.
/tpc -: Cancels a teleport request.
/sethome <name> -: Creates a home.
/home remove <name> -: Removes a home.
/home list -: Lists all homes in your current world.
/home help -: Shows home help menu.
/m -: Opens market place.
/red shop -: Redeem items.
/fmarket -: Opens farmers market.
/buy -: Lists all vehicles (vehicles last the whole wipe).
/<vehicle> -: Buys and spawns a specific vehicle.
/ibag -: Opens ingredients bag.
/cook -: Opens cooking menu.
/backpack -: Opens backpack.
/buyraid -: Purchases a raid base.
/rb -: Views raid statistics.
/buydungeon -: Purchases a dungeon.
/share <player> -: Shares an object with a specific player. If no player name is included, it shares the object with everyone.
/unshare <player> -: Stops sharing an object with a specific player. If no player name is included, it unshares the object from everyone, removing all access.
/sharelist -: Lists your shares.
/shareclear -: Clears all your shares.
/checkit -: Checks the share status of an object (look at it and type the command).
/remove -: Removes an object (look at it and left-click an object).
/limit -: Shows your building/item limits for that specific world.
/toolazytowalk -: Summons a free bike.
/boatrecover -: Recovers your boat (ONLY works in deep sea).
/jet -: Activates jetpack.
/stfix -: Fixes skill tree bugs (like the crafting table skill).
